Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect the details you give us when you join the Market2Door waitlist. Last updated 4 November 2026.

Who we are

Market2Door (“we”, “us”) is preparing to launch an app in Nigeria for foodstuff delivery, market sourcing, parcel delivery and van and truck hire. For the waitlist on market2door.ng, we are the data controller. You can contact us at support@market2door.ng.

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • If you choose to tell us: how you’re interested in Market2Door (as a customer, rider, van or truck owner, or business) and your state.

To protect the form from abuse, we also keep a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address for up to 24 hours. We don’t store the IP address itself.

Why we use it

  • To keep your place on the waitlist and tell you when Market2Door is ready in your area.
  • To contact riders, vehicle owners and businesses about working with us when applications open.
  • To send you updates about the launch. Every update email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To understand where demand is, so we can plan where to open.

We use your details because you gave us your consent when you joined. You can withdraw it at any time, and doing so doesn’t affect anything we did before.

Who can see it

Only the Market2Door team. Your details are stored on our web hosting provider’s servers and sent by email through our own mail service. We don’t sell your details, and we don’t share them with advertisers.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while the waitlist is running and until we launch in your area. After that, we either welcome you as a customer or partner with your agreement, or delete your waitlist record. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you can ask us to:

  • show you the details we hold about you, or send you a copy;
  • correct anything that’s wrong;
  • delete your details;
  • stop contacting you, or stop using your details for a particular purpose.

Email support@market2door.ng and we’ll respond as quickly as we can. If you’re not happy with how we’ve handled your details, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Keeping it safe

The waitlist is stored in a database that can’t be reached from the web. Only signed-in team members can view it, over an encrypted connection.

This notice covers the waitlist only. When the Market2Door app launches, it will have its own privacy policy covering orders, payments and deliveries.
