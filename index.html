About us

Welcome to Market2Door

Market2Door is creating a smarter way to shop for foodstuff, send packages, move goods and access reliable logistics services, all through one connected platform.

Nigerian markets have the freshest food and the best prices. They also have the traffic, the heat, the haggling and the heavy loads. Our team knows the markets, works with trusted traders and keeps you in control of every price, so you get the market without the stress.

Four services, one appFrom the market to your door

Our services: everything you need in one place

Market2Door brings the market, the rider and the truck together. These are the services we’re building, and each one opens as it’s ready.

  • Foodstuff marketplace

    Fresh produce, rice, beans, yam, garri, grains, oils, spices, provisions, meat and fish, in the units the market sells.

  • Managed market sourcing

    Send your list. Our sourcing team checks availability, quality and price with trusted traders before anything is bought.

  • Parcel delivery

    Book a pickup with an approved rider, get a quote first, follow each update and receive proof of delivery.

  • Van & truck hire

    Goods movement, home and office relocation and bulk transport, with tailored quotes for bigger jobs.

  • Business services

    Bulk products, storefronts and secure logistics integrations for approved businesses, with every business account kept separate.

Why Market2Door?

  1. 01

    You approve every change

    Market prices move. If a price, quantity or item changes, or we suggest a substitute, nothing is charged or delivered until you approve it.

  2. 02

    Honest pricing

    Every item is clearly marked as a fixed price, an estimate confirmed at the market, or a quote, so an estimate never looks like a final price.

  3. 03

    Safe from market to door

    Quality checked at the market, carried by approved riders and drivers, and only marked as paid once our payment provider confirms it.
